About Desby

We're building for the way it already works.

Desby started with a simple observation: Nigerian fashion is already a massive industry. It just wasn't built with the systems to match.

We saw talented tailors running serious businesses from notebooks, WhatsApp chats, memory and spreadsheets. Measurements got lost. Orders became difficult to track. Payments got messy. And apprentices learned the craft without always having a clear path to becoming business owners.

We knew because we had lived it.

So we built Desby, an operating system for the people who make fashion happen.

Desby brings the everyday realities of Nigerian fashion into one place, helping fashion businesses manage their clients, orders, payments, production and growth.

Who Desby is for

One platform, every hand in fashion

Fill your calendar, price with confidence, keep every client's measurements forever, and get discovered by new customers browsing designs.
